## In-App Logos

zzzbench bundles no logos. `zzzbench --logos` reports that artwork is
unavailable; `--logo NAME` is rejected before device discovery. Benchmarking
does not require artwork, and rendering fixtures are synthetic.

## Adding Artwork

Before adding any image or logo, record here its exact source, the license or
owner permission that allows publication, modifications, and required notices.
The source code license does not grant rights to project or third-party marks.
